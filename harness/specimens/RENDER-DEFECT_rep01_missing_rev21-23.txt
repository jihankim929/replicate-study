SPECIMEN — the default that left one replicate three charter revisions behind.
Filed 2026-08-31 on the PI's REPORT 007 ruling. Read-only record; nothing here is live code.

THE LINE
--------
harness/rerender_charter.py, in main():

    reps = sys.argv[1:]
    if not reps:
        reps = [i for i in C.RATIFIED["phases"]["main"]["ids"] if i != "rep01"]

`rerender_charter.py` is the tool that pushes an amended charter into workspaces that are already
provisioned. Its no-argument default is every main-phase replicate EXCEPT rep01. Run without
arguments -- which is how a fleet-wide amendment is naturally applied -- it silently amends
fifteen of sixteen.

WHY THE EXCLUSION EXISTED, AND WHY THAT STOPPED BEING TRUE
---------------------------------------------------------
rep01 was the launch-gate replicate: provisioned, verified and started alone, ahead of waves A
and B. At the moment the exclusion was written rep01 had just been provisioned from current
source and did not need re-rendering. That was true once. It was not re-examined at the next
amendment, and the exclusion is unconditional -- it does not test whether rep01's charter is
current, it tests only that the replicate is rep01.

MEASURED CONSEQUENCE
--------------------
Revisions 21, 22 and 23 were ratified and rendered on 2026-08-29. rep01 did not receive them.
Measured 2026-08-31 by diffing rep01's live CHARTER.md against a fresh render of the same arm:

rep01 worked under that text from 2026-08-29 until 2026-08-31, roughly two days of a seven-day
campaign.

WHY IT MATTERS BEYOND BOOKKEEPING
---------------------------------
Two of the three missing revisions were ruled COMMON CORE, both arms identically -- so the
omission put one replicate on a different charter from the other fifteen along an axis the study
deliberately holds constant.

One of them is about cost. "Cost mechanics and discipline" states that session cost is dominated
by accumulated context re-read every turn, sets norms for how tool output enters a session, and
ends: "The spend meter in your workspace shows your position against the budget; consult it when
planning." rep01 never had that clause and is the highest spender in the study and the first
replicate to approach its $280 cap -- $194.84 of $280, 69.6 percent, at 04:01 KST on 2026-08-31,
against a fleet mean near 47 percent.

That is a correlation in a sample of one and it is NOT offered as a cause. It is filed because
the confound is now in the record either way: rep01's spend behaviour cannot afterwards be read
as purely a property of rep01.

DISPOSITION
-----------
Revisions 21-23 delivered to rep01 2026-08-31 on PI ruling, with an INBOX notice stating plainly
that they are late and why, disclosed as a mid-campaign correction. The default in
rerender_charter.py is left AS IS in this specimen for the record; whether it is changed is a
separate ruling, since a tool whose default silently omits a replicate is the defect and removing
the literal is not the only possible fix.
