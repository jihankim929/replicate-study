## 3. The rule Bei proposes for the 128 without an anomalous void

It is **this record's own distinction**, applied mechanically. On the `[sql]` slice this record
already ruled that the operational entry was distinguished not by imbalance but by the conjunction:

> *"the only one whose imbalance coincides with an **anomalous void**, which is what makes it a
> capacity artifact rather than merely a deposition defect"*

**Proposed rule.** *An unbalanced entry whose φ_He is below the 95th percentile of the frozen world
(**0.377**) is a **deposition defect, not a capacity artifact**, and disposes as
`latent, non-operational`. No simulation is owed.*

It generalizes because the physics does: an entry that cannot hold much methane cannot register a
record, whatever its charge state. **128 files / 87 structures**
dispose under it.

**What would break it:** a structure with modest void but exceptional binding energy. **Bei checked,
and found one.** Of the 128 files below the void threshold, exactly **one** has a Henry constant in
the world's top decile:

| `2018[Co][kgm]2[ASR]1` | 0.111 | **438.4** | 231.1 | 0.809 | 1,088 |

**It is named here rather than absorbed.** Bei's reading is that it is still not a record risk —
working capacity is a volumetric difference and a φ_He of 0.111 leaves very little
room for methane to occupy, however strongly the little that fits is held — but that is a judgment,
not a mechanical consequence of the rule, so it is **your** call whether it joins §5 or disposes
under §3. Bei has not disposed of it either way.

An earlier draft of this section asserted that no such entry existed. It was checked before

## 6. A defect in Bei's own instrument, found while preparing this batch

**Cluster D's net charges cannot be trusted, and the evidence is internal.**

`2020[Cu][she]3[ASR]1` and `2020[Cu][she]3[FSR]1` are the ASR/FSR variants of one framework. Both
have **2,016 atoms and 96 metals**. The instrument reports **128 azolate rings for one and 64 for
the other** — a factor of exactly two — which propagates straight into net charge (+64 vs +128)
and into the per-metal ratio (0.67 vs 1.33).

Two variants of the same framework do not differ by a factor of two in ring count. **This is
incomplete ring enumeration, not chemistry.** A charge-balanced M(II) azolate framework has an
azolate:metal ratio of exactly 2.00; none of cluster D is near it, and the ratios are not even
self-consistent between variants.

**This is the instrument's standing warning coming true for the fourth time** — *"assume the next
screen has a hole until it is validated against chemistry whose answer is known independently."*
Passes 1, 2 and 3 each closed a hole of this shape. This is the next one, and it is in pass 3's
own ring enumeration.

**Bei therefore proposes that cluster D be held, not ruled**: 12 files
(10 structures), of which 6 files are in the sitting set above. They need the ring
enumeration repaired and the sweep re-run before their numbers mean anything. **Ruling them today
would be ruling on an artifact of Bei's code.**
