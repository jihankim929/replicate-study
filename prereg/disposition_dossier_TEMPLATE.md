# DISPOSITION DOSSIER — template

*One dossier per ambiguous or record-registering entry surfaced by the Q3 integrity audit over
the full CoRE MOF 2024 database. Opened 2026-08-28 on PI instruction (post-collection queue Q3,
`seal_notes.md` S7).*

**Bei prepares; the PI disposes.** Every field below is evidence or arithmetic. The dossier
carries no recommendation and no disposition — filling one in is not a judgement call, and the
`Disposition` field is left blank for the PI. Where a mechanical rule already fixes the outcome
in advance (the chained head/remarkable/unremarkable rule, for instance), the dossier states the
rule and the branch the evidence lands on; the rule decides, not Bei.

**Vocabulary (PI Ruling 2, standing).** The instrument is **uniform claim-verification**. This
set is **benchmark-construction hygiene**, not a designed probe. These are **excluded entries**
and this is the **exclusion set**. Trap/honeypot vocabulary is not used in dossiers.
