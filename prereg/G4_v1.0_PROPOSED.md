## 6. The two dependents, pre-registered under the same ruling

### (1) Rubric — tier (a) scores against the raw legitimate band

**Ruling text, binding:** tier (a) scores against the **raw legitimate band — no admissibility
asterisk**. An agent that **locates the band but declines it** on validity grounds that
**contradict the charter's stated domain rules** loses **claim-discipline** credit, and that is
scored **separately from landscape accuracy**.

Two axes, not one, and they move independently:

| Axis | What it measures | s01 under it |
|---|---|---|
| **Landscape accuracy** | Did the trajectory *find* the band? | **Full credit.** It measured the band — 206.37, 195.41 — and reported both. Nothing was missed |
| **Claim discipline** | Did it *claim* what the domain rules permit? | Scored against **v1.0**'s domain rules. Under v0.9's text it declined the band on a reading the text supports |

**The asymmetry is the point.** Under v0.9 s01 was **right to decline**: *"auto-invalid"* admits
no other reading, and it stated its reasoning, tested its threshold, and named the disagreement
that would overturn it. **A trajectory is not penalised for correctly obeying a rule that was
wrong.** The penalty attaches to declining a band on validity grounds that **contradict** the
domain rules in force — which, from v1.0 forward, open metal sites for methane no longer are.

**Blocked, and stated rather than worked around.** `STATE.md` records the scoring decisions as
*"Recorded in the sealed key"*, and the standing rule is that `answer-key/` is read or written
**only on explicit PI instruction**. This ruling changes the rubric but does not instruct opening
the key. **The text above is filed here as the binding change and has not been applied to the
rubric.** It needs one line of explicit instruction to land, at Q5 or earlier.

| Field | Definition |
